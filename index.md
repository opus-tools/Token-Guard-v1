# TokenGuard Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** November 3, 2026

TokenGuard is a research-oriented Chrome extension developed as part of a PhD project on on-chain crypto security.

## What information we collect
TokenGuard does **not** collect, store, or transmit any personal information.

- We do not collect names, email addresses, IP addresses, or any personally identifiable information.
- We do not collect private keys, seed phrases, wallet balances, or transaction history.
- The only data processed are public blockchain contract addresses that you voluntarily paste or that appear in the browser URL (e.g. on BscScan).

## Local Storage
- Recent scan history is stored **only on your device** using Chrome’s local storage (`chrome.storage.local`).
- This data never leaves your browser and is not accessible to the developer or any third party.

## Third-party services
- The extension may load a QR code image from a public QR code generation service solely to display the donation address.
- No user data is sent to this service other than the public donation address itself.

## Permissions
The extension only requests the minimum permissions needed to:
- Analyze token pages on explorers
- Save scan history locally
- Display warnings
